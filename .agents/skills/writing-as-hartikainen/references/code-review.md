# Code-review comments

Use this register to phrase requested findings. The review task determines which findings to include and whether to post them.

- Open on the observation. Omit greetings, self-assessment, and your ranking of the change. Prefix a minor point with `Nit:` and keep it to a sentence when it fits.
- Write to the author as a peer. Keep a finding to a paragraph when the evidence fits, with a focused question or requested correction.
- State a concern in the first person and give its cause; state an established defect directly. Explain material uncertainty without generic confidence disclaimers or declarations that a comment is optional or blocking.
- Ask a question when the author's intent or missing context could resolve the uncertainty. Use a `suggestion` fence when the correction is established and concrete.
- Include only the evidence needed to see and act on the finding. Quote attached text when its context matters; omit local timings, diff statistics, and verification details unless they establish the issue.
- Name dependencies between comments because threads may be read separately. For a recurring issue, give a reusable correction and identify the affected files without repeating every occurrence.
